
# Water Leak Detect UAE

## Website Overview

Water Leak Detect UAE is a Dubai-based water leak detection and plumbing service provider serving residential, commercial and industrial customers. Its services include residential and commercial leak detection, underground pipeline detection, swimming pool leak detection, plumbing repairs and emergency plumbing services.

The redesigned homepage focuses on presenting the company's specialist services more clearly, improving customer navigation and establishing a more professional and credible first impression.

## Three Main Improvements

### 1. Stronger First Impression and Consistent Homepage Messaging

**Previous issue:** The original homepage contained duplicated hero content, placeholder text, spelling errors such as "DISCOBER MORE" and generic messaging that weakened the company's professional presentation.

**Implemented improvement:**
- Addressed duplicated and inconsistent homepage content.
- Replaced placeholder and incorrect text with clearer messaging.
- Strengthened the presentation of the company's water leak detection and plumbing services.

**Result:** A more professional homepage with clearer business positioning and messaging.

### 2. Improved Service Structure and Customer Navigation

**Previous issue:** Six leak detection services and nine plumbing services were presented without sufficient distinction, making it difficult for different customer groups to identify relevant solutions.

**Implemented improvement:**
- Organised leak detection and plumbing services into distinct categories.
- Improved the presentation of individual service offerings.
- Created a clearer content structure for residential, commercial and industrial customers.

**Result:** Visitors can navigate the service offerings more easily and identify the solutions relevant to their property or business.

### 3. Clearer Presentation of Technical Expertise

**Previous issue:** The website made broad claims about advanced technology, 15 years of experience and 24-hour emergency services without sufficient supporting technical information or project evidence.

**Implemented improvement:**
- Improved the presentation of the company's specialist service capabilities.
- Focused messaging on actual leak detection and plumbing services.
- Avoided relying on unverified experience claims as established facts.
- Prioritised clear and credible business information.

**Result:** A more transparent presentation of the company's expertise without overstating unsupported claims.

## Design Improvements

The redesigned homepage introduces a more organised and consistent visual presentation.

Key improvements include:

- **Visual presentation:** Replaced duplicated and placeholder content with a cleaner, more professional homepage structure.
- **Layout:** Improved content organisation and separation between leak detection and plumbing services.
- **User experience:** Made service categories and customer navigation clearer.
- **Content consistency:** Improved messaging, corrected text issues and strengthened the company's specialist positioning.
- **Business credibility:** Focused on communicating actual services rather than relying on unsupported claims.
- **Customer accessibility:** Structured information to help residential, commercial and industrial visitors identify relevant services and contact the company.
