Start with the signs

A leak is not always where the water appears.

Damp patches, unexplained water bills and changes in water pressure can point to a hidden leak. The visible symptom may be some distance from the actual pipe problem. A specialist assessment helps determine what needs to be investigated.

01 / INSIDE

Damp walls, ceilings or floors

Water marks, damp areas and unexplained moisture can indicate a leak within internal pipework.

02 / OUTSIDE

Unexpectedly high water bills

A leak on an incoming mains supply pipe may continue unnoticed while water escapes underground.

03 / UNCERTAIN

You know something is wrong

If the source is unclear, a conversation with the team can help establish the appropriate next step.

Detection methods

The right method depends on the leak.

Different leaks leave different clues. Miracle Leak Detection describes using a combination of inspection and specialist equipment to investigate suspected water leaks.

The method selected depends on factors such as pipe material, pipe location and the conditions around the suspected leak.

✳ Why this matters: understanding the likely source before access can help keep the investigation focused.
Explore Detection Technology
METHOD / 01

Acoustic detection

Listening equipment can help identify sounds associated with escaping water in pipework.

METHOD / 02

Thermal imaging

Infrared imaging can highlight temperature variations that may help identify a suspected leak area.

METHOD / 03

Tracer gas

A specialist tracing method described by the company for investigating certain concealed leaks.

METHOD / 04

Moisture mapping

Moisture meters can help identify areas with unusual moisture levels in building materials.

METHOD / 05

Endoscopic cameras

Small inspection cameras can help examine certain concealed spaces and difficult-to-reach areas.

METHOD / 06

Visual inspection

Initial inspection can identify visible pipe defects, dampness and other signs that guide further investigation.
